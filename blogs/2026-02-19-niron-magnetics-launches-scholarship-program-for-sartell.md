MINNEAPOLIS, Minn. (February 19, 2026) – Niron Magnetics, a leading manufacturer of rare-earth-free Iron Nitride permanent magnets, today announced it is working with the Sartell-St. Stephen Dollars for Scholars program to award scholarships supporting local students pursuing higher education and technical training.
